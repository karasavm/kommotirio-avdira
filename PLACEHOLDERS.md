## Privacy / GDPR note

The site currently uses **no tracking, no analytics, no embeds** — so no cookie banner is legally required. If you add Google Analytics, an embedded Google Map iframe, or a Facebook pixel later, you will need a cookie consent mechanism and a privacy policy page. Consult a legal advisor for GDPR/ePrivacy compliance specific to your situation.
